The heavy hitters

Minerals, ruminants and fossil plumbing top the list.

Cement and lime release CO₂ from the rock itself when limestone is heated, and cattle emit methane by digesting. Neither is solved by cleaner electricity, which is why they sit at the top.

The per-dollar trap

Per dollar, ships look like the dirtiest way to move freight.

Deep-sea freight shows — kg per dollar against — for rail. Shipping is cheap per tonne moved, so a dollar buys far more transport by sea. Spend factors rank how carbon-intensive the money is, not how carbon-intensive the service is.

So what

Use it as a flashlight, not a scale.

Spend factors are good at one job: finding the few purchases that hold most of the carbon. They are poor at comparing suppliers, modes or products, because price moves the result as much as emissions do. Find the hotspots with this table, then replace those lines with supplier or physical data.
